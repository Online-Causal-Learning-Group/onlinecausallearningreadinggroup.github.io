Online Causal Learning Reading Group

Fall 2026 Semester

We meet weekly to discuss recent papers in theoretical statistics, causal inference, and adaptive experimental design. Open to all interested graduate students and researchers.

There are lots of open problems in the intersection of causal inference + adaptive design, and going through a good text book will go a long way. The material is very easy to read and no prior knowledge is required.

Location: Zoom

Preferred Day: Thursday or Friday (Every week)
