Online Causal Learning Reading Group

Fall 2026 Semester

Our weekly reading group explores recent literature in theoretical statistics, causal inference, and adaptive experimental design.

The intersection of causal inference and adaptive design offers many exciting open problems, and working through a foundational textbook together is an excellent way to build our collective understanding. This page serves as our central hub for schedules, readings, and resources.
